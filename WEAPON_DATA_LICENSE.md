`shared/weapon-catalog.mjs` transcribes the 38 weapons in the SRD 5.2.1 Weapons table (pages 91–92), including their damage, properties, mastery, weight, and cost. Source: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf. Class proficiencies and mastery progression follow the same document's class tables. The app bundles this data; it does not fetch weapon data while playing.

Changes: the table was converted to structured fields, properties and mastery effects are summarized, and descriptions are assembled for display. Character-owned weapons allow custom names, statistics, proficiency/ability overrides, quantities, ammunition, and limited uses. No paid weapon catalog or magic-item collection is included. This notice covers the included SRD material, not application code.
